Good to know

Student & new-grad FAQ

Do I need prior professional experience?

No. Our early-career pathways are built for students and recent graduates. We hire for curiosity and character, then teach the rest through mentorship and real work.

Are internships paid?

We'll cover specifics for each opening during your intro call. Reach out with the track you're interested in and we'll walk you through the details.

Can an internship turn into a full-time role?

Yes. Strong performers frequently grow into full-time positions and defined career tracks — early-career hiring is one of the main ways we build our teams.

Which track should I apply to if I'm unsure?

Apply to the one that interests you most, or ask about the new-grad rotation — it's designed to help you explore several practice areas before you commit.

Ready to start?

Email your résumé and a short note about the pathway and track you're interested in. Every application is read by a real person, and we aim to respond within one business day.
