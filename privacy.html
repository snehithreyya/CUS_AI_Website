Privacy

Privacy Policy in plain language.

What this site, its forms, and the AI assistant do with your information — and what they don't.

1. Who we are

CUS Solution is a US-based technology consulting and staffing company. This policy explains how this website (the “Site”), its contact and booking forms, and the CUS AI Assistant handle information about you. Questions? Email info@cussolution.com or call +1 (307) 313-5867.

2. Information you give us

We only receive information you choose to send us:

  • →Contact and booking forms. Name, email, phone, company, topic, and the message you write. These forms open your own email app with a pre-filled message addressed to info@cussolution.com — nothing is sent until you press Send in that app, and the Site itself does not store your entries on a server.
  • →Direct email or phone. Anything you send us, including résumés for career inquiries.
  • →AI Assistant chat. Messages you type are answered from a knowledge base built into the Site and run in your browser. If a retrieval backend is enabled in future, this policy will be updated to say what is sent to it.

Please do not put sensitive personal data (government ID numbers, financial account numbers, health information) in forms or chat.

3. How we use it

We use what you send to respond to your inquiry, schedule consultations, evaluate career applications, and — for client and candidate relationships — to deliver the services you asked about. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-party services on the Site

To render pages, your browser requests resources from third parties: the Tailwind CSS CDN (styling) and Google Fonts (typography). Those providers receive standard request data such as your IP address and browser details under their own policies. Links to LinkedIn and Instagram take you to sites we do not control. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

5. Cookies and tracking

The Site does not currently run advertising or analytics trackers. If we add analytics later, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent.

6. Retention and your choices

We keep correspondence only as long as needed for the purpose it was sent. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information you have sent us by emailing info@cussolution.com; we will respond within a reasonable time. Residents of some US states and other regions have additional statutory rights, which we will honor as applicable.

7. Children

The Site is intended for businesses and job seekers, and is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Site evolves; the date below will change when we do.

Last updated: November 9, 2026. This is a plain-language summary of our practices and is not legal advice.
