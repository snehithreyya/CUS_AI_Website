How We Hire

A clear, respectful process

Four straightforward steps — typically wrapped up in one to two weeks, with feedback at every stage.

01

Apply

Send your résumé and a short note about the work you want to do. Every application is read by a person.

02

Intro call

A relaxed conversation about your background, goals, and what you're looking for — and your questions for us.

03

Skills interview

A practical, role-relevant discussion or exercise — no trick questions, just how you actually think and build.

04

Offer & onboarding

A clear offer, then a structured onboarding with your mentor and first project mapped out from day one.
