About CUS Solution

Who we are

What does CUS Solution do?+

CUS Solution is a US-based technology consulting and staffing company. We deliver software development, cloud solutions, engineering, data, and business consulting projects, while also building in-house technology products and connecting skilled professionals with industry opportunities.

Where is CUS Solution based?+

CUS Solution is a US-based company. We work with clients and professionals across the country and support remote, hybrid, and on-site engagements depending on project needs. You can reach us at info@cussolution.com or +1 (307) 313-5867.

Why aren't your clients listed publicly?+

Many of our client relationships are governed by confidentiality agreements. To protect client privacy and proprietary information, we do not publicly disclose client names without authorization.

Services & Engagements

How we work

What services does CUS Solution offer?+

We deliver four core practices: Technology Consulting, Software Development, Cloud Solutions, and Engineering Services. Across these we support data analytics, cybersecurity, DevOps and SRE, enterprise modernization, and business systems work.

What types of technology projects do you deliver?+

We support software development, cloud computing, data analytics, cybersecurity, supply chain, mechanical and industrial engineering, business systems, and enterprise technology modernization initiatives.

Do your consultants work on real client projects?+

Yes. Our professionals contribute to active client engagements as well as internally developed technology projects, depending on business needs and project assignments.

How does a typical engagement start?+

It begins with a short scoping conversation to understand your goals, timeline, and constraints. From there we propose an approach and team shape, agree on scope and success measures, and start delivery — adjusting as we learn. You can book a consultation or email info@cussolution.com to begin.

What engagement models do you offer?+

We support project-based delivery, staff augmentation, and contract, contract-to-hire, and direct-hire staffing. The right model depends on your timeline, in-house capacity, and whether you need a full delivery team or specific skills alongside your own.

How much does an engagement cost?+

Pricing depends on scope, engagement model, team size, and duration, so we scope each engagement individually rather than list fixed rates. Share your requirements and we'll provide a tailored estimate — typically within one business day.

How long does a project take?+

Timelines are scope-dependent. Focused efforts can run a few weeks, while modernization or platform builds run longer and are usually sequenced into value-delivering increments. We give a realistic timeline once we understand the goals in a scoping call.

Hiring & Careers

Talent & opportunities

How do I hire talent through CUS Solution?+

Share your hiring requirements through the Contact page or ask the AI assistant to get started. We discuss the roles, skills, and timeline, then match qualified professionals and agree on the engagement model that fits — contract, contract-to-hire, or direct hire.

Do you work with students and new graduates?+

Yes. Our Students & New Grads program helps early-career professionals build real-world experience on meaningful work. Visit the Students page or the Careers page to learn about opportunities and how to express interest.

How do I apply for a career opportunity?+

Visit the Careers page to learn about roles and how we work, then use the Contact page to share your interest and background. You can also ask the AI assistant to point you to the right next step.

Which industries do you serve?+

We work across multiple industries, applying technology consulting, software, cloud, and engineering capabilities to each sector's needs. See the Industries page for the verticals we support.
